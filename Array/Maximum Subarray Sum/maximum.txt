It is the maximum sum which a subarray can give from a given array.

In case of only positive umber subarray: It is the sum of all the numbers in an array.

int a[5] = {1,2,3,4,5};
its sum would be 15 which is its maximum sum.

But in case if an array includes both positive and negative number then we have 3 approch:

1. Brute Force Approach
2. Slightly Optimized Approach
3. Kandane's Algorithm
